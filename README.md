My entry for the [One Word for the Boss](https://make.fun/bounty/u-swift-badger-44-one-word-for-the-boss-6b48c735) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun): exactly one word describing your boss — no phrase, no caption, no explanation. The word has to land on its own.

> **Bottleneck**

Every team has one: the person every decision, approval and email has to squeeze through before anything moves. Funny because it's true, and true in every kitchen, warehouse and office.

`main.py` prints the word and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
